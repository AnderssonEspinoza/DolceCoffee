# Dolce Coffee

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Product Purpose
A Spanish-language café website with a product catalogue, café introduction, opinions, contact and account entry pages. Repository: AnderssonEspinoza/DolceCoffee.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript. Keep all eight existing entry URLs. Original catalogue has nine coffee entries at $12.99 and six food entries at $13.99. Product names are descriptive labels derived from the original images, pending owner confirmation. No backend, authentication, checkout or contact delivery exists. Local selection is browser-only; do not imply order fulfilment.

## Evidence on Hand
Existing product PNGs and barista photographs in assets/images. Original story and review copy was Lorem ipsum; no verified testimonials, address, contact details, origin, opening hours or sourcing claims are available. Do not invent them.

## Brand Commitments
Dolce Coffee name, coffee and sweet/savory food offering. User requested a complete, more impressive redesign using design skills. The user confirmed that the café serves everyday moments of connection, reflection, listening to music with headphones, enjoying coffee, sharing with a partner or friends, and advancing personal tasks in a welcoming place. It is not positioned around glamour.


## Users and experience — confirmed by the owner

People seeking a warm, fully felt café experience: a place to think about their day, listen to their own music, enjoy their drink, share time with friends or a partner, or make progress on their own things. Warmth should come from hospitality, people and the experience, not a prescribed cream or pastel palette. The owner approved publishing the existing design with the Emil Kowalski refinements. Three exploratory directions are preserved separately in the `demos/dolce-propuestas` branch for future studio demos; they are not part of the Dolce Coffee deployment.
